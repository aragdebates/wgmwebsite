What we do

What does
WGM do?

01

Debate camp meetings.

We meet in person during the summer at national camps, bringing together debaters from 100+ schools.

 ↘ 02

Speaker panels.

We interview and hold panels with prominent women in debate to share their experiences, advice, and perspectives.

 ↘ 03

A support network.

Our blog, meetings, and organization make space for shared experience, honest reflection, and connection with zero judgement.

 ↗ 04

Mentor–mentee program

A program matching older debaters with younger debaters, providing strong women and gender minority role models.

 ↘

Summer Sessions

Debate Camp Meetings

Our summer meetings bring women and gender minorities together at debate camps together for conversation, support, and a chance to connect across schools. Everyone is welcome & we always have snacks!
